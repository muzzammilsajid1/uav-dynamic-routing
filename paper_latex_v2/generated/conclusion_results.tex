Under the shared graph, timing, and information contracts, the classical planners provide the strongest reliability and scale in this benchmark. DDQN+HER is competitive only on the native small-grid setting: it achieves 96.0\% held-out-pair success and 90.0\% on unseen matched-density layouts, but falls to 0.0\% at $100\times100$ while all classical planners remain at 100\%. The ablations further reject a simple component story: Double DQN and local observations help, whereas HER, shaping, and curriculum are not individually supported as beneficial under the tested protocol. The contribution is therefore not a claim of RL superiority, but a reproducible map of where learned routing works, where it fails, and which conclusions survive seed variation, paired scenarios, distribution shift, scale, and event-level analysis.
