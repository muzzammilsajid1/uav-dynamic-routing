Across five independently trained policies, DDQN+HER achieved 96.0\% mean success on held-out endpoints, 90.0\% on unseen matched-density layouts, and 72.0\% on denser layouts; A*, Dijkstra, and D* Lite achieved 100\% on all three splits. RL success fell from 98.0\% at $15\times15$ to 0.0\% at $100\times100$, where route decision time was 932.266 ms versus 3.566 ms for A*. Post-change RL success was 87.9\%, despite the recovery criterion being met before termination in all observed events. Ablations were non-monotonic: removing HER, shaping, or curriculum each reached 100\% held-out-pair success.
