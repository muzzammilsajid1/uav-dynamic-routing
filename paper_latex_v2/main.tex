\begin{abstract}
Claims of learned routing superiority can depend on baseline choice, training
seed, and test distribution. We therefore compare Dijkstra, A*, D* Lite, and
Double DQN with Hindsight Experience Replay (DDQN+HER) on one persisted
310-scenario suite with identical movement, obstacle, information, and timing
contracts. Seven RL configurations are trained with five policy seeds
(35 runs; 17.5 million environment steps) and evaluated on held-out endpoints,
layout and dynamics shifts, four grid sizes, and controlled realism factors.
Route timings are repeated ten times, and change events are analyzed through
cost shock, recovery, reaction work, and post-change success.
